What Is Cowork?

Cowork puts the agent that powers Claude Code into the Claude desktop app, with no terminal to learn. You hand it a multi-step job and it does the work while you supervise. This program is built entirely on Claude Cowork from Anthropic, and grounded in Anthropic's own guidance for using it well.

Foundations & Personal Value · ~20 min
01 — Why we're here

Why Gen Re is investing in Cowork

You didn't get a licence because someone wants you typing faster. You got one because the work that fills your week — reading long documents, reconciling numbers across them, and writing up what you found — is exactly the work a supervised agent can carry the first mile of. The three days are about making that first mile reliable enough to trust.

Time back on document-heavy work

Treaty submissions, claims files, quarterly packs, board memos. The reading, the cross-referencing, and the reformatting are hours you don't get back and don't get credit for. Cowork does that first pass in minutes so your hours go to the call you're actually paid to make.

Your judgment stays in the loop

Cowork helps you check things, not whip through things. Its best trick isn't speed — it's a second reader that never gets bored: it will re-add a column of figures, notice a date that contradicts the one on page four, and tell you which document each claim came from.

Manager-level licences mean you own verification

These licences went to people who already sign off on work that leaves the building. Nothing about Cowork changes who is accountable for what goes out. Cowork drafts, you verify, you sign. Everything in these three days is built to make that verification quick rather than optional.

02 — Rules of the road

Four rules for the next three days

These are the ground rules for the room, not corporate policy in miniature. They exist so nothing you try today can create a problem you have to explain later.

1
Work only in the workshop folder: Desktop\cowork-workshop.
Everything Cowork touches lives there as ordinary files, so you can open the folder in Explorer and see exactly what changed — nothing happens somewhere you can't look.
2
Keep confidential cedent and client material out of this pilot. Use the sample documents we ship instead.
Our client contracts govern what may leave Gen Re systems, and a training exercise is the wrong place to test that line. The samples are built to behave like the real thing.
3
Review every output before it goes anywhere. That means a colleague's inbox, a client, or a shared drive.
Cowork produces confident prose whether or not the underlying number is right. You are the control, and that is the job the licence came with.
You can name the source document each claim came from.
4
When something doesn't work or doesn't make sense, say so in the room.
Interesting tangents get parked on the list for later; blockers get solved now, while facilitators are standing three feet away. Nobody gets further by quietly falling behind.
03 — Chat vs. Cowork

How Cowork is different from a chat

In a chat you go back and forth on a question and then copy the answer somewhere useful. In Cowork you describe the result you want and Claude works across your files to produce it, checking in as it goes. The output is usually a finished document or spreadsheet rather than a transcript you still have to act on.

In a chat

You and Claude talk through a problem. It can't open your files, so anything it suggests, you carry out yourself. That's a good fit for thinking out loud, drafting, and quick answers.

In a Cowork session

You give Claude a goal and access to a set of files. It reads them, edits and creates what it needs, and hands the work back for you to review.

💡
Why people miss this at first

Anthropic's own guidance puts it bluntly: without training, people use Cowork the way they use chat and never see the difference. Helping your team make that shift is where most of the value comes from, and it's what the rest of this program is about.

04 — Where it runs

Local, with guardrails

Claude Cowork is the Claude Code agent inside the Claude desktop app on macOS and Windows, made for people who don't write code. It plans the work, splits it into steps, and can run several streams at once. It does all of that on your own machine.

Your files

It only touches folders you grant

Claude reads, edits, and creates files inside the folders you connect, and nothing outside them. It also checks with you before it deletes anything permanently. You control its reach by choosing the folder.

The app has to stay open and the machine awake while a task runs.
Code in a sandbox

It runs code in an isolated VM

When a task needs code or a shell command, Cowork runs it in a separate virtual machine on your computer. That's how it can write working Excel formulas, decks, and documents without having free rein over your system.

06 — Where it fits your day

Good first tasks at Gen Re

The fastest way to feel the difference is to hand off something real and document-heavy. These are the shapes of work that pay off first for this cohort — every one of them producing a draft you review and sign off, the same way you would a junior colleague's.

Summarize

Turn a long submission, treaty wording, or claims file into a one-page brief for the people who won't read the original — with every figure and statement pointing back to the page it came from.

Draft

Take your notes, a data pack, or an email thread and draft the memo, the update, or the file note in your format and your voice. The structure rarely changes, which is what makes it an ideal early win.

Check

Ask it to reconcile the totals, compare this quarter's pack against last quarter's, and list what doesn't agree. Tedious, detail-heavy checking is where a tireless second reader earns its keep.
