Stage 2 · Workshop content

How this session works

There are no lessons attached to this block, on purpose. The content is whatever you bring.

How the session runs

Fifteen minutes to level-set what everyone brought, twenty to go round the room and hear the individual use cases, then forty-five working them — screens shared, hands on keyboards, the Nimble Gravity team moving between people. We work the queue by whatever is most blocked, not by who spoke first.

What makes a good bring-along

Three things: the real files, so we're not working from a description; a workflow you actually own, so you can tell whether the output is right; and one question you're stuck on, stated plainly. A vague "how could I use this?" gets a vague answer — a broken skill you can open on screen gets fixed.

What happens to what we don't finish

Ninety minutes will not clear every case. Anything unresolved is written down in the last ten minutes as a named follow-up action with an owner and a route — office hours, a direct follow-up with the Nimble Gravity team, or something Gen Re needs to turn on first. Nothing leaves the room as "we'll see."

✓
What you'll leave with

A deeper understanding and resolution of issues with individual use cases.

Bring, don't download

There is no file pack for this session. Bring your own machine, your own folder, and the case you want worked on. If your files live on OneDrive or SharePoint, copy them local before you come — sync delays eat working time and look like Cowork failing when they aren't.
