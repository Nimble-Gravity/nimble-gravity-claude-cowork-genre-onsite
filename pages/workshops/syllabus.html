Day 3 · Thursday afternoon · 2 hours

Gen Re Use Cases + Governance Snapshot

The first of Thursday's two blocks, and the one everyone attends. Open the hub →

Objective

In this session, Cowork finds your use cases for you: you'll install the ng-brainstormer skill, sit a short interview about your role, and get back a ranked slate of ten candidate skills with the payoff arithmetic done. One item from a room slate gets built into a working skill live, and you leave with a trimmed governance and rollout snapshot to take back to your team.

Pre-work required · 15 min

Spend ten minutes noting what fills your week — Thursday's ideation is run by a skill that interviews you about your role, live in the room. No canvas to fill in; just bring a laptop with Cowork working. Full pre-work →
