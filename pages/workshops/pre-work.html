Pre-work & Follow-up

Three days, and every one of them has pre-work. It is short — 30–45 minutes before Days 1 and 2, fifteen before Day 3 — but it is required, because each session opens with everyone already installed, already holding the same folder, and already knowing which piece of their own work they want to look at. Day 1 is the long one. Do it first.

Day 1 · Tuesday · Foundations & Personal Value

Install, unzip, and read two things

Pre-work required — 30–45 minutes. Five steps on your own machine, then two short reads. If any of it fails, say so before Day 1 rather than on the morning — a room of thirty people is a bad place to fix an install.

1
Install Claude Desktop and sign in with your Gen Re account.
Everything across the three days happens in this one app. There is no website version of what we're doing.
The app opens and you see Cowork in the left-hand rail, not just a chat box.
2
Download the workshop folder: workshop-folder.zip.
It holds every file used across the three days — the demo document, the Day 2 files, and the folder your personalization gets written into.
The file lands in your Downloads folder and is called workshop-folder.zip.
3
Right-click the downloaded file and choose Extract All…
A zip file is a box with files packed inside it. Nothing can read the files while they're still in the box — "Extract All" unpacks the box and gives you a normal folder.
A small window opens asking where to put the unpacked files.
4
In that window, change the destination to your Desktop, then click Extract.
Everything we say for three days points at one place: Desktop\cowork-workshop. If it lives somewhere else, every instruction is slightly wrong for you.
The window closes and a folder appears on your Desktop.
5
Open Desktop\cowork-workshop and confirm what's inside it.
Checking now takes ten seconds. Discovering an empty folder at 9:05 on Tuesday costs you the first segment.
You can see about-me/, demo/, day-2/, README.docx, and a file called claude.md. If you see a folder called workshop-folder with another folder inside it, drag the inner cowork-workshop folder out onto the Desktop and delete the rest.
6
Read What is Claude Cowork, and why is Gen Re using it?
Ten minutes. What Cowork is, how it differs from a chat window, and what it is for here. The session opens assuming you've read it.
7
Review the Rules of the Road.
What data may and may not go into Cowork. It is the ground rule underneath everything else in the three days, and it is shorter than this paragraph suggests.
📦
If you've never unzipped a file — that's fine, here's the whole idea

A zip is a box of files squashed into one file so it's easy to send. You can usually peek inside it by double-clicking, but the files aren't really on your computer yet — they're still in the box, and programs like Claude can't reliably use them there. Extract All is the "unpack the box" button: right-click the zip, choose Extract All…, tell it where to put the contents, and you get an ordinary folder you can open like any other. The original zip can be deleted afterwards; you don't need it again.

Day 2 · Wednesday · Skills & Everyday Workflows

Pick the piece of your week you want to look at

Pre-work required — 30–45 minutes, via this portal. Two short reads and one decision. The decision is the one that matters.

1 · Review the Day 2 lessons

Twenty minutes on Walk a Workflow and Skill Anatomy. We cover both again in the room; the room moves faster if you've seen them once.

2 · Confirm your everyday workflow

One piece of recurring work you do yourself — inbox triage, a weekly status report, a monthly recap. It needs to repeat, and you need to know what a good version of it looks like, because you'll be the one judging the output.

3 · Bring your Day 1 results

The personalization files the interview wrote for you, still in your cowork-workshop folder, on the same laptop. We check in on them together at the top of the session — including whose didn't take, and why.

Day 3 · Advanced · Build Your Own

Bring the thing you're stuck on

Pre-work required — 15–30 minutes. Come prepared with use case examples or questions that need resolved. No other prep needed.

Write the problem down

One paragraph is plenty. "It doesn't fire" and "the output isn't good enough" are both fine starting points — as long as you can open the actual case on screen.

Bring the real files

Working from a description wastes the block. If your files live on OneDrive or SharePoint, copy them local before you come — sync delays eat working time and look like Cowork failing when they aren't.

Check you're on the list

Attendance is by invitation, for experienced Cowork users. If you're not sure whether you're in this block, ask before Thursday rather than on the day. Details on the Advanced hub.
