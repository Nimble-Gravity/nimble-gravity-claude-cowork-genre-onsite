Run of show

The 2-hour agenda

The demo runs twice — before the lab and after it — because the difference between the two runs is the point of the session.

0:00–0:1515 min
Open
Why Gen Re is investing in Cowork and what it means for your daily work

The case for change in the room's own terms, and the honest version of what this does and doesn't change about your job. See Lesson 1 §Why.

0:15–0:3015 min
Teach
Rules of the road: what's safe, what isn't, and how to work confidently

Four rules for the three days, and the data ground rule underneath them. Lesson 1 §Rules · Rules of the Road.

0:30–0:5020 min
Teach
Chat vs. Cowork: what each is for and when to use it
  • What Is Cowork? — delegating a job versus asking a question
  • Get Set Up — same build, same model, and what personalization produces
  • The Workshop Folder — hand over one folder, confirm Cowork can see it
0:50–1:0515 min
Demo
Live demo: the treaty-layer summary, before the lab

Hands off keyboards. The facilitator asks a Cowork that knows nothing about anyone for a one-page summary of the demo document, and the room reads the answer against the source. See Lesson 4 §Before.

Facilitator: don't narrate over the run. Show, then talk.

1:05–1:5045 min
Lab
Hands-on lab: set up your personalization (About Me and folder structure) · open the lab →
  • Point Cowork at your workshop folder and start the interview
  • Answer as yourself — role, voice, priorities, and what you won't put your name to
  • Confirm the files exist, then run the verification prompt and check what comes back

Facilitator: circulate; hold explicit silent working periods and show each step twice before turning the room loose.

1:50–2:0010 min
Demo
Live demo: the same summary, after the lab

Same document, same question, personalized machine. The room names what changed and what didn't. Then the Day 1 knowledge check at the end of the lesson, the applied task, office hours, and a preview of Day 2. Close with a 2-minute feedback.
