Deep dives (reference only)

Not part of the onsite — kept for self-study

None of the material below is part of the three-day program, and nothing in the sessions assumes you've read it. It comes from a longer four-workshop version of this curriculum aimed at IT and platform teams. It stays here because some of it is genuinely useful once you've got the basics — particularly if you end up being the person your team asks about rollout and controls.

Using Cowork

Decompose Your Workflow — mapping a workflow into steps, data sources, and a picture of good versus bad output.

Make It a Skill — turning prompt text that already works into a skill, and the skill-creator loop that hardens it.

A caution on dates

The governance and analytics pages carry facts verified in July 2026, and those facts move. If you're going to repeat something from them in front of someone who matters, check it first — or ask us.
