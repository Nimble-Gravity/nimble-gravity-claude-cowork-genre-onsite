Gen Re · Day 3 of 3

Gen Re Use Cases + Governance Snapshot

In this session, Cowork finds your use cases for you. You'll install the ng-brainstormer skill, sit a short interview about your role, and get back a ranked slate of ten candidate skills with the payoff arithmetic done — then watch one item from a room slate get built into a working skill live, and leave with a trimmed governance and rollout snapshot to take back to your team.

🛟
Backup demo — facilitators

If the live build stalls, the facilitator runs the prepared demo skill instead of debugging on the projector. The prepared skill, the files it runs on, and the point at which to make the call are all decided in advance. Deciding to switch is not a failure of the session; a fifteen-minute silent debug in front of the room is.

📁
The skill, and the lab files

The session's one download is ng-brainstormer.skill — a zip; the folder inside goes in your Cowork skills folder (install steps in Lesson 1). The live build then runs on the files that come with the chosen slate item, staged in a folder everyone can reach. Bring the same cowork-workshop folder you've used all week — your deck lands inside it.
