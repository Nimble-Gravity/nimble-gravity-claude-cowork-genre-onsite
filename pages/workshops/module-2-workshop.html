Run of show

The 2-hour agenda

The order is deliberate: the manual walk comes before any talk of skill anatomy, because the steps you had to remember are the argument for writing them down.

0:00–0:1010 min
Open
Connect & frame

Recap Day 1, objectives for today. Quick check-in on everyone's personalization results — including whose didn't take, and why.

0:10–0:3525 min
Manual
Walk a real business workflow (e.g., status reporting) · open the walkthrough →
  • Five steps, done by hand, with you remembering every one of them
  • Read the draft against the sources — the habit that makes the output usable
  • Notice which parts you had to carry in your head

Facilitator: manual first. Do not teach skill anatomy before the room has felt the manual version.

0:35–0:5520 min
Teach
What a skill is and how a few stack together
  • Getting the job out of your head and onto a page
  • The description does most of the work — why a skill fires, or doesn't
  • A small, finished skill, then how two of them chain through one ask

Facilitator: keep the terminology simple; skip skill vs. agent vs. connector unless someone asks.

0:55–1:3540 min
Lab
The Skill Vault · enter the vault →
  • Room 1 · The Inherited Skill — run one somebody else wrote
  • Room 2 · The Broken Trigger — a skill that never fires, because of its description
  • Room 3 · Forge Your Own Key — write one from scratch
  • Room 4 · The Playbook Door — chain two of them through a single ask

Facilitator: one room for business users, facilitators roam. Keep the facilitator view open for live team progress and resets; the second hint costs a team two minutes.

1:35–1:5015 min
Debrief
Show results, discuss good vs. so-so skill

One share per team, then the comparison that matters: what separates a skill that fires and holds up from one that technically works. Then the Day 2 knowledge check at the end of the lesson.

1:50–2:0010 min
Close
Homework & close

The applied task, office hours, and a preview of Day 3 — including the use-case canvas you'll pre-fill before Thursday. See your homework, then a 2-minute feedback.
