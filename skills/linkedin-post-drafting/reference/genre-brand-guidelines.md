## Typography

- Sans-serif throughout — modern, clean. No serif or display faces observed.
- Bold, larger weights for headings; consistent, readable body proportions.
- No specific typeface name could be confirmed from the public site alone.

## Overall visual tone

Conservative and corporate, with modern refinements — structured layouts, clear hierarchy,
professional photography (executives), financial-strength messaging, formal service descriptions
balanced with accessible language. The aesthetic reads as an established financial-services firm:
stability and expertise over trend-following design.

**Directly consistent with the tone/voice guide** (`genre-tone-voice.md`) — restraint and
credibility over flash, on the visual side as much as the written one.

## Logo and tagline

- Wordmark logo, clean corporate typeface, appears in header and footer as SVG.
- Tagline: **"The People Behind the Promise®"** — note the registered trademark symbol; carry it
  through if the tagline is used verbatim in generated content.
