## Day 1 – Workshop 1

Welcome to Workshop 1 of the Claude Cowork onsite series for Gen Re business leaders.

**Objective:** By the end of this session, every participant will have set up their own Claude personalization and seen it work on a real, genericized Gen Re document — and will know how to verify Claude's output.

All materials are on the Gen Re Cowork portal: **Link to be supplied by Derrikk**

- Review the Rules of the Road
- Come ready to participate. This is a lab, not a lecture.

**Subject:** Claude Cowork Onsite Workshop — Day 2: Skills & Everyday Workflows

**Agenda (2 hours)**

| Segment | Time | Topic |
|---|---|---|

## Facilitator Notes

### Standing rules — all three days

- Build in deliberate pauses for processing
- Repeat or paraphrase key points rather than assuming one pass lands
- Lead with "what to do," then explain "why"
- Defer off-topic tangents to offline follow-up

### Workshop 1

- Separate "watch me do it" from "now you do it" — don't narrate over a live demo
- Show each step at least twice before turning people loose
- Build in explicit ~5-minute silent work periods for the personalization exercise
- Give a simple test to confirm personalization worked (e.g., "ask Claude X, you should see Y")
- Skip the legal-plugin example

### Workshop 2

- One room for business users; facilitators roam
- Sequence is manual first, then skill — don't teach skill anatomy before showing the workflow
- Keep terminology simple; skip skill vs. agent vs. connector unless someone asks
- Flag OneDrive/SharePoint sync delays if the demo touches a shared file

### Workshop 3

- Validate use cases with Chris/Charles beforehand — some IT-oriented ideas (like status roll-ups) may not land with execs who receive reports rather than compile them
- This is the longest day — build in a real break, not just a pause
- Have a backup demo ready in case live-building stalls
