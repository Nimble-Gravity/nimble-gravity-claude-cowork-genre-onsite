THE PROGRAMME

Meridian Mutual is a mutual property and casualty writer operating across seven midwestern states, with a book weighted towards homeowners and small commercial property. This is the fourth consecutive year we have participated on their catastrophe programme.

The 2026 placement is a four-layer property catastrophe excess-of-loss treaty attaching at $2,500,000, with total programme limit of $32,500,000 above the retention. Our signed share is 22.5% across all four layers, unchanged from 2025. Reinstatement provisions are one paid reinstatement on Layers 1 and 2, and free reinstatement on Layers 3 and 4. All figures below are 100% of the treaty, not our share.

LOSS ACTIVITY IN THE QUARTER

Two events were notified in the period, both convective storm.

The first, a severe thunderstorm and hail outbreak across the Missouri valley on 14 April 2026, was advised at $6,400,000 gross to the cedent as at 30 June. That takes the retention in full, exhausts Layer 1, and puts $1,400,000 into Layer 2. Meridian's adjusters had closed roughly 60% of the claim count by the end of the quarter, and the reserve has moved twice since first advice, both times upwards. We should expect further development here.

The second, a hail event across the northern portion of the book on 27 May 2026, is advised at $2,180,000 gross. That sits inside the $2,500,000 retention and produces no recovery under the treaty unless it develops. Movement to date has been modest.

Layers 3 and 4 are untouched, as they were in each of the previous three treaty years.

Against $4.41 million of ceded premium for the year, incurred of $3.90 million at the halfway point puts the treaty at a loss ratio of 89% on a written basis — before any allowance for development on the April event, and before the second half of the wind season. On a full-year view this treaty is unlikely to make money.

POINTS FOR THE REVIEW

- Layer 1 is exhausted, so the single paid reinstatement on that layer is now triggered. Treaty Accounting is calculating the reinstatement premium; it is not in the figures above and will need to be before the year-end position is struck.
- The April reserve has moved upwards twice on partial claim closure. Any further development goes straight into Layer 2, which has $3,600,000 of unused limit.
- The cedent's advice on both events came through later than the 30-day notification standard in Article 9. Not material this quarter, but it is the second year running.
- Meridian Mutual has told the broker they are considering raising their retention to $5,000,000 at renewal. That would remove Layer 1 from the programme, which is where the premium is.
- Aggregate exposure across our midwestern cedents is close to the level that triggers a monitoring review. S. Okafor is running the test for the Q3 cycle.
