MUNICH RE — Q2 2026 RESULTS SUMMARY (investor update excerpt)

Munich Re reported second-quarter 2026 net profit of approximately €2.2 billion,
bringing first-half 2026 net income to about €3.9 billion — already more than 60%
of the full-year earnings target.

The company reaffirmed its full-year 2026 net income target of €6.3 billion, while
trimming its 2026 reinsurance revenue guidance by €2 billion to €38 billion.

On April 1 treaty renewals, written volume fell 18.5% to €2.0 billion as Munich Re
walked away from business that did not meet its pricing or terms thresholds.

CFO commentary: "Pricing remained favourable despite a softer April renewal."
Management attributed the strong first half to disciplined underwriting, strong
investment income, and broad earnings contributions across the business mix.

Source: Munich Re half-year 2026 financial report and related investor coverage,
published August 2026.
