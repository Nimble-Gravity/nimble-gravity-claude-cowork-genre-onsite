BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY — Q2 2026 10-Q, INSURANCE SEGMENT EXCERPT

Berkshire Hathaway's insurance operations generated pre-tax underwriting earnings
of $2.18 billion in Q2 2026, down 14.0% year-over-year. The decline was driven by
GEICO, whose pre-tax underwriting income fell 45.4% YoY to $994 million.

Berkshire Hathaway Primary Group: pre-tax underwriting earnings of $273 million,
up from $63 million in Q2 2025.

P&C Reinsurance: pre-tax underwriting earnings grew 8.9% year-over-year to
$1.14 billion. Per the filing, this segment "offers excess-of-loss and quota-share
reinsurance coverages on property and casualty risks through the NICO, General Re
and TransRe Groups" — reported as a single combined figure across all three units.

No underwriting result, combined ratio, or premium figure is broken out for
General Re individually anywhere in this disclosure.

Source: Berkshire Hathaway Inc. Form 10-Q, Q2 2026, and related coverage of the
insurance segment results, published August 2026.
