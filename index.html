What You'll Be Able to Do

From a first setup to a working skill

Cowork takes the agent behind Claude Code and makes it usable without a terminal: you describe a result and it works across the folders you hand it to produce one. Over three days you set it up as yourself, walk a real piece of your own week through it, and build a skill from a Gen Re workflow — with the governance answer you'll be asked for on the way out.

Who it's for

Gen Re managers and business leaders — actuarial, underwriting, claims, finance. No prior agentic-AI experience assumed, and nothing here requires you to write code. If you own a recurring piece of work and know what a good version of it looks like, you're the audience.

What you leave with

Your own personalization files, a working skill built from a real Gen Re workflow, a way to check whether what Cowork hands back is actually supported by the source — and short, honest answers to the questions your team will ask you on Friday.

How it runs

What to expect in the room

The same shape every day, so you always know where you are and what's being asked of you.

01
Pre-work, required
30–45 minutes before Days 1 and 2, fifteen before Day 3. Short, but not optional — the sessions open assuming it's done. It's all on the pre-work page.
02
What to do, then why
Every step is shown before it's explained, and shown at least twice. We pause deliberately rather than talking over the screen, and tangents get parked for offline follow-up.
03
Hands on keyboards
Roughly half of each day is you working on your own laptop, on your own files, with facilitators moving around the room. Bring the machine you actually work on.
04
Hybrid ground rules
The room's audio doesn't carry remote voices, so remote attendees get moderated Q&A: post questions in the chat and a named moderator reads them into the room. Say your question there rather than waiting for a gap.
